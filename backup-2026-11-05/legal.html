2. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone, business name (if any), saved addresses, notification preferences.
  • Order details: pickup/drop-off addresses, receiver contact, package description/weight and payment status. Paystack processes card details; this website does not store card numbers, PINs or one-time passwords. We store transaction references and payment verification/status information.
  • Messages you send via forms or WhatsApp.

Delivery alerts

Where enabled for your booking, email or SMS alerts may be sent to the contact details provided. Alert availability depends on system configuration and communications networks; check the tracking page or contact us for shipment updates.

Live rider location sharing

For bookings using the existing tracking workflow, an assigned rider may share live GPS location while a trip is active. Location is visible through the tracking link and to authorised staff, and riders can start or stop sharing. Anyone with the tracking link may view available shipment data; share it only with people you trust.

How we use & share data

  • To pick up, route, deliver, communicate and support your orders; to prevent fraud; and to improve our service.
  • Your rider receives the pickup/drop-off details and phone numbers needed for the job.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (hosting/database/login), Paystack (online payment processing), UPS (tracking for UPS-carried shipments), our SMS provider, email service, and maps providers (OpenStreetMap/Google Maps when you open directions).
  • We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data where required by Nigerian law.

Storage, security & your rights

  • Data is stored in access-controlled cloud systems. No system is 100% secure — please use a strong password.
  • You may view/edit your profile and saved addresses in your dashboard, and request account deletion there. Shipment records may be retained as needed for business, legal and dispute purposes.
  • For privacy requests, contact us via the Contact page or WhatsApp 0802 431 1091.

Cookies/local storage

We use essential login storage (Firebase) and may remember recent shipment references on your device for quick tracking.

Weight, dimensions and other shipment details may affect carrier acceptance and the quotation.
